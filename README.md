# Bountiful — Complete Village Bounties for Special Loot

Bountiful 9minecraft Mod brings a fun quest system to your world with wooden bounty boards. You can find these boards hanging on houses in villages or craft one for your own base. The boards give you simple jobs like gathering wood, hunting zombies, or fishing up rare books. When you finish a task, you get paid in items, experience points, and special coins. It gives you a great reason to keep exploring and working without changing how the base game feels.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Ejektaflex**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
